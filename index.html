Add bin days to your phone

Choose your phone to get started. It’s free, and you only need to set it up once.

Apple / iPhoneAndroid

Apple / iPhone

  1. Tap Add to Apple Calendar below.
  2. When Calendar opens, choose Subscribe, then confirm or tap Add. The wording may vary on your phone.
  3. Keep event alerts enabled if that option appears.
Add to Apple Calendar

Your bin days will appear in Apple Calendar. If nothing opens, use the calendar address under “Need another way?” below to add a subscribed calendar in your Calendar settings.

Android / Google Calendar

Google needs this one-time setup in a web browser on a computer. Sign in with the same Google account you use on your phone.

  1. Tap Copy calendar address below.
  2. On your computer, open Google Calendar’s “From URL” page. If you copied the address on your phone, open this webpage on your computer to copy it there too.
  3. Paste the address and select Add calendar.
  4. Open Google Calendar on your phone. Tap ☰ and tick Brookvale Bins. It may take a little while to appear.

A heads-up the day before

Each bin day includes a reminder for the previous day. Some calendar apps, including Google Calendar, may ignore reminders supplied with a subscribed calendar.

To check yours, open your calendar’s settings and look for Brookvale Bins → Notifications. If available, add an all-day event notification, 1 day before, and choose a time that suits you. Make sure your phone allows calendar notifications too.

If your app doesn’t offer alerts for subscribed calendars, set a weekly Tuesday reminder on your phone to check which bin is due. Adding this calendar alone cannot guarantee a phone notification.

What if a bin day changes?

Once subscribed, your calendar can pick up corrections automatically. Updates may take a day or longer to appear, depending on your calendar app. Check local collection notices for last-minute changes.
